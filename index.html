Hi, I'm Caleb.

I'm a sports photographer based in South Jordan, Utah, and I started Captured by Caleb Carter to give athletes and families photos worth keeping long after the season ends.

I also work part-time in construction, installing fire sprinkler systems. That job taught me to show up early, work in any weather, and get the details right the first time. I bring the same habits to every sideline.

I'm also a student at Ensign College, studying communications with a focus on digital marketing, social media, and content creation. School is where I learn how great photos turn into stories that teams, schools, and athletes can actually use.
